Our printing

In order to fulfil our missions, PT KKM is committed to always improve our manufacturing technologies to achieve optimal efficiency and effectiveness. To date, we are already producing various types of products, such as printed motives fabric (batik), sarong, shawl, bed sheets, pillowcase and bolster, and table cloth using various types of materials, such as cotton, rayon, polyester, and a mixture of cotton polyester and polyester rayon. Our manufacturing facilities are supported by sophisticated machines and technologies from Asia and Europe, and expert production personnel. Currently, PT KKM is using a flat printing machine to produce our highest quality products.
