Products

Printing

To fulfil our mission, PT KKM is committed to continually developing and upgrading our manufacturing technology to achieve optimal efficiency and effectiveness. We produce a wide range of products, including printed fabrics, sarongs, shawls, bed sheets, pillowcases and bolsters, and tablecloths, using materials such as cotton, rayon, polyester, and cotton-polyester and polyester-rayon blends. Our manufacturing facilities are supported by advanced machinery and technology from Japan, Korea, China, and Europe, as well as skilled production personnel. PT KKM uses flat and rotary printing machines to produce high-quality products.
