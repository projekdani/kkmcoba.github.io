About Us

Starting from a husband and wife from Pekalongan who had a dream being able to create quality batik cloth, in 1990s, a batik printing production business was established under the name of CV Argo Peni. Initially only producing traditional batik motifs, now continues to develop to produce modern textile batik motifs. Consistency and Integrity are the main keys in the business development. After surviving the economic crisis 1997 - 1998 and significant fire in their factory in 2004, in 2006, they established PT Kusuma Kencana Mulya (KKM) with a vision to become the most trusted and most sought-after integrated textile company in Indonesia.
