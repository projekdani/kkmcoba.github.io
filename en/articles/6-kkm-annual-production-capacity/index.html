PT Kusuma Kencana Mulya Production Capacity: Over 10 Million Meters of Fabric a Year

In the textile industry, production capacity is an important factor in meeting customer needs, especially for brands that require large quantities of fabric and a well-planned production process.

PT Kusuma Kencana Mulya (KKM) supports its production with modern textile printing machinery and technology from Asia and Europe.

With this technology, PT Kusuma Kencana Mulya can produce more than 10 million meters of fabric and 5 million sarongs every year.

Capacity for a Wide Range of Needs

Substantial production capacity allows PT KKM to support textile requirements for a variety of applications. From fabric to sarongs, every production process is part of the effort to meet customer needs.

With modern textile printing machinery, the company continues production to serve diverse market demand.

Trusted by Local and International Customers

Production capacity and technological support are part of PT Kusuma Kencana Mulya's ability to serve its customers. Customers in local and international markets trust the company to meet their textile product needs.

This trust is an important part of the company's ongoing commitment to supporting the evolving textile industry.

Bring Your Fabric Ideas to Life with PT KKM

Every brand has different needs and product concepts. Choosing the right production partner is therefore an important step in turning ideas into textile products.

Have a fabric idea or concept you would like to bring to life? Discuss your production needs with PT Kusuma Kencana Mulya and find a solution that suits your brand.
