Printed Batik vs. Stamped and Hand-Drawn Batik

Hand-drawn batik, stamped batik, and printed batik each have their own characteristics and advantages. Understanding the differences helps textile businesses and brands choose a production method that suits their needs.

What Are the Benefits of Printed Batik?

1. Clear Colors and Pattern Details

Printed batik allows designs to be printed with sharp details and colors that match the concept. The result depends on the design, fabric type, ink, and printing process.

2. Design Flexibility

A wide range of patterns can be developed, from traditional motifs to modern designs with varied shapes and colors. This gives brands the flexibility to establish a distinctive visual identity for their products.

3. Consistency in Mass Production

Printing makes it possible to repeat the same pattern, helping maintain design consistency across large production runs.
