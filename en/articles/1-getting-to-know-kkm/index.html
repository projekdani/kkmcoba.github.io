Getting to Know Kusuma Kencana Mulya and the Story of Textile Printing in Pekalongan

The fabrics we use every day have a long story before they become finished products. Behind each pattern, color, and texture is a production process that calls for experience, precision, and consistency.

PT Kusuma Kencana Mulya (KKM) is a textile company focused on printed batik production and has been growing in Pekalongan for decades. Starting as a printed batik fabric producer, KKM has expanded from traditional batik patterns to a wide range of modern textile and batik designs.

Our Roots in Printed Batik

Pekalongan is known for its long history in the batik industry. It is the place where KKM's journey began.

Over time, demand for patterned fabrics has diversified—not only for batik, but also for fashion and a wide range of other textile products.

This growth has driven KKM to continually develop its production capabilities, technology, and people to meet increasingly diverse textile needs.

Process Is Part of Quality

Making fabric is about more than printing a pattern onto a material.

Before fabric is ready to use, it passes through several important stages, from material selection and design development to color matching, printing, and quality inspection.

Each stage plays a role in determining the finished fabric.

That is why production experience is essential to maintaining consistent processes and product quality.

Growing with the Textile Industry

KKM continues to develop its manufacturing technology and production capabilities. Its facilities are supported by machinery and technology from Asia and Europe, as well as a skilled team directly involved in production.

For KKM, progress in the textile industry is about more than keeping up with technological change. It also means continually improving, understanding customer needs, and maintaining quality at every stage.

From Pekalongan, that journey continues.

Because behind every fabric are the process, experience, and people that bring it to life.
