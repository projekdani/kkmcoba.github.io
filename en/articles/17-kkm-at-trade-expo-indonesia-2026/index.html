PT Kusuma Kencana Mulya at Trade Expo Indonesia 2026

PT Kusuma Kencana Mulya participated in Trade Expo Indonesia (TEI) 2026, held on October 14–18, 2026, at the Indonesia Convention Exhibition (ICE) in BSD City, Tangerang. The event was an opportunity to showcase printed fabric and expand business connections in the textile industry.

Trade Expo Indonesia is a trade exhibition that brings together businesses, manufacturers, buyers, and business partners from around the world. It creates opportunities to exchange ideas, build partnerships, and introduce Indonesian products to a wider market.

For PT Kusuma Kencana Mulya, taking part in the exhibition is a way to showcase its printed fabric production capabilities and explore new collaboration opportunities. By meeting visitors and prospective business partners, the company can present its product range and discuss fabric requirements based on concepts and market demand.

Printed fabric offers a wide range of possibilities, from batik patterns to designs for fashion. With flexible design options and attention to pattern details and color accuracy, PT Kusuma Kencana Mulya aims to support customers as they develop textile products.

Taking part in Trade Expo Indonesia 2026 is about more than exhibiting; it is an opportunity to build connections, expand collaboration, and introduce Indonesian textile products to a wider market.

PT Kusuma Kencana Mulya hopes its participation will create new opportunities for collaboration and strengthen relationships with business partners from Indonesia and abroad.
